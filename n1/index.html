ESC

None required.

The Deep Blue 50R has an integrated SMU (System Management Unit) that acts as the motor controller. It handles everything an ESC would: converting battery DC to motor AC, managing throttle, reporting faults. No external ESC is needed.

Removes one component entirely. Simplifies wiring.

APD 200F3[X] v2 (previously in BOM) is rated for 60V max. Deep Blue runs at 350V. Connecting them would destroy the APD instantly. Removed.

Hull

Planing catamaran, hydrofoil-capable design

Twin-pontoon GRP or carbon fibre catamaran. Two hulls provide stability, separate battery compartments per pontoon, and mounting surface for one Deep Blue 50R per hull stern.

ParameterValue

SteeringRudder (one per hull, servo-actuated), not differential thrust

Why rudder and not differential thrust: differential thrust reduces speed when turning, one motor slows to steer. Rudder keeps both motors at full power and deflects water flow for direction change. Critical for a speed-sensitive mission.

Why Hydrofoil

Hydrofoil is required to hit 100 km range at 35-50 km/h. Conventional hull cannot do both simultaneously.

A hydrofoil is an underwater wing attached to struts below the hull. As the boat speeds up, the wing generates lift. Above roughly 25 km/h the hull lifts completely out of the water. Only the thin struts remain submerged. Drag drops by approximately 80%.

Without hydrofoil (conventional planing hull):

Energy consumption from Torqeedo official data: At 10 km/h (1/4 power, one motor, one i3 pack): 80 km range = 0.47 kWh/km Scaled for 2 motors + 2 packs (~1,000 kg vessel): At 10 km/h -> 160 km (but takes 16 hours) At 20 km/h -> 40 km At 35 km/h -> 18 km At 42 km/h -> 9 km <- full speed, mission fails

With hydrofoil (80% drag reduction):

Reference: Candela C-7 (1,100 kg, 40 kWh usable, 92 km at 41 km/h on hydrofoils) Energy per km: 40 / 92 = 0.435 kWh/km at 41 km/h Scaled for our vessel (750 kg foiling, 75.8 kWh usable): At 45 km/h -> ~115 km [ok] At 50 km/h -> ~95 km [borderline] Sprint phase check (final 25 km at 65 km/h): Remaining after 75 km cruise: ~28 kWh Power at sprint: ~1.4 kWh/km -> 28 / 1.4 = 20 km Slightly short -> reduce cruise speed to 42 km/h to save 8 kWh for sprint

Hydrofoil + Deep Blue 50R integration challenge: when hull lifts 40-60 cm on foils, outboard leg must remain submerged. Deep Blue 50R long shaft = 63.5 cm. Foil height and motor mounting must be engineered to keep propeller submerged during foil flight. Not plug-and-play.

Candela C-8 reference: 1,300 kg, 75 kW motor, 69 kWh, 105 km at 41 km/h. Proves 100+ km at 40+ km/h is achievable with hydrofoil on comparable weight class.

Communications

Starlink Mini (primary) + Holybro P900 (terminal backup)

Starlink Mini is the primary link for all video, telemetry, and control commands.

Why Starlink and not RF (P900 / Walksnail): Radio horizon at 1m antenna height = 3.57 x sqrt(1) = 3.6 km Combined horizon (boat + ground station at 2m) = ~8-10 km At sea surface: max range is ~8-10 km regardless of frequency or brand Mission range = 100 km -> RF fails after first 10 km Starlink = satellite at 550 km altitude -> no horizon problem
ComponentRoleRange
Starlink MiniPrimary: video + MAVLink + RC over internetUnlimited
Holybro P900RF backup telemetry, terminal approach only~8-10 km surface

Starlink can be jammed near defended targets. P900 serves as backup for the final 8-10 km if satellite is disrupted. ArduPilot continues autonomously on GPS if both are jammed.

Starlink Mini specs: $199 hardware, 25-40W power draw, 20-40ms latency, 80-150 Mbps, 12V DC.
